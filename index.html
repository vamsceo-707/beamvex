DWG 02Scope of Work

Detailing services, from model to fabrication floor.

Every deliverable is built to your connection standards and issued against a revision-controlled drawing register — so what leaves our shop matches what gets welded on site.

01

Structural Steel Detailing

Full 3D modeling and shop/erection drawings for primary structural frames, complete with connection design coordination.

02

Miscellaneous Steel

Stairs, handrails, ladders, platforms and embeds detailed to code — the small members that hold up an inspection.

03

Connection Design

PE-stamped connection calculations across US, Canadian, and Australian codes, delivered alongside the detail set.

04

BIM / 3D Coordination

Federated Tekla and Revit models with clash detection, ready to hand off into your BIM 360 or ACC environment.

05

Erection Drawings

Sequenced erection plans and piece marks written for the crew on the ground, not just the model in the office.

06

Material Take-off & NC1/2

Accurate bill of materials and DXF/NC files for CNC fabrication, cut lists, and procurement sign-off.

DWG 03Workflow

From RFQ to
issued-for-construction.

A fixed five-stage workflow, the same on every job — so your PM always knows exactly where the drawing set stands.

01

Takeoff

We review IFC drawings, specs, and connection standards, and scope the job with you before modeling starts.

02

Modeling

Full 3D model built in Tekla or SDS/2, checked against loads and clearances as it's built, not after.

03

Connection Check

Connections verified against your governing code, with calcs available on request.

04

Drawing Issue

Shop and erection drawings issued for review with a full revision log and RFI tracking.

05

Revise & Release

Markups turned around fast, drawings released IFC/IFF with a clean audit trail.
